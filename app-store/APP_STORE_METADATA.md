## English (United States)

**Name:** Grocery Benefits Tracker

**Subtitle:** SNAP, WIC & Budget Planner

**Promotional text:** Track SNAP/PAN balances, WIC benefits and grocery budgets. Scan UPC/EAN barcodes, plan split payments and keep core records on your iPhone—no account or benefit PIN.

**Keywords:** EBT,PAN,food stamps,barcode,UPC,shopping,expense,spending,balance,allowance,cash,offline,history

**Description:**

Grocery Benefits Tracker is an independent, manual organizer for people who plan grocery purchases with SNAP or Puerto Rico PAN, WIC and cash. Enter the balances and benefits you want to track, build a basket, assign payment methods, and see how each completed checkout affects your own records.

PLAN EACH GROCERY TRIP
• Scan supported UPC/EAN barcodes and use the bundled offline item index, or enter items manually.
• Build a basket with store, item, brand, category, quantity and price details.
• Mark SNAP eligibility as eligible, not eligible or unsure.
• Assign SNAP/PAN, WIC, cash or a split payment before checkout.
• Save baskets to finish or reuse later.

TRACK BENEFITS AND BUDGETS
• Track one or more SNAP/PAN cards with manual balance changes and reloads.
• Record WIC benefits by category, unit and available quantity, with optional dates and authorized-brand notes.
• Copy selected WIC benefits into a new benefit period.
• Set weekly, biweekly, monthly or custom shopping budgets and review period history.
• Schedule optional local reminders for tracked balances and WIC dates.

REVIEW YOUR OWN RECORDS
• Search completed transaction history and record corrections.
• View reports for benefit activity, spending, stores, grocery categories, budget periods and monthly trends.
• Export reports as PDF, XLSX or CSV, with masked-value options.
• Move History between phones with encrypted or unencrypted backups.
• Temporarily mask financial values and WIC quantities with Privacy Mode.

English (United States) and Spanish (Puerto Rico) are included.

PRIVACY AND PURCHASES
No sign-in is required. The app never asks for an EBT/WIC PIN and does not connect to a government benefit account. Core tracker records are stored in the app on this device. Files you choose to export or share are handled through your selected destination.

Without an active one-time Remove Ads purchase, the app may show one fixed non-personalized banner. There is no subscription. Google AdMob may process device and advertising data as explained in the Privacy Policy.

IMPORTANT
This is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the USDA Food and Nutrition Administration (FNA; formerly FNS), Puerto Rico ADSEF, any SNAP/PAN or WIC agency, retailer or card issuer. It does not provide official balances, eligibility decisions, retailer acceptance or product authorization. Official sources control.

## App Review notes

Grocery Benefits Tracker is an independent manual tracker. It does not authenticate with, retrieve information from, or modify any government benefit account. All SNAP/PAN balances, WIC benefits, shopping items and budgets displayed in the app are entered manually by the user. The app never requests an EBT/WIC PIN, full benefit-card number or government-account login.

No sign-in or demo account is required. On first launch, choose English or Spanish, select SNAP—United States or PAN—Puerto Rico, choose the benefit types to track, and complete or defer the optional benefit and budget setup. Review the Terms and Privacy Policy, confirm the two required acknowledgements, and tap “Agree & open app.”

To test the core workflow:
1. Open Cards & Budgets and enter a manual SNAP/PAN balance, WIC benefit or shopping budget.
2. Open Shop and tap Scan. Camera permission is requested only at that point. Scan a supported UPC/EAN barcode, or cancel/deny camera access and enter the item manually. A recognized code may be matched locally against the bundled offline USDA FoodData Central-derived index.
3. Choose SNAP/PAN, WIC, Cash or a supported split allocation, and complete checkout.
4. Review the resulting local record in History and Reports.
5. Reports can be exported as PDF, XLSX or CSV. History can be transferred with an encrypted or unencrypted user-initiated backup.
6. Optional local notifications are requested only after the user explicitly enables a reminder.

In-app purchase: `remove_ads_lifetime` is a one-time non-consumable. Open Remove Ads in the sidebar to purchase. Use Settings → Restore Purchase to restore a verified prior purchase. There is no subscription. StoreKit supplies the localized price.

Without a verified Remove Ads entitlement, the app may show one fixed non-personalized Google AdMob banner after Google’s applicable UMP process and Apple App Tracking Transparency resolution. Denying ATT does not restrict app functionality. A banner might not appear if Google reports that an ad request is unavailable or returns no fill. Settings → Report an Ad opens the localized support route.

Core tracker records remain in the app on the device and are not sent as advertising parameters. User-initiated exports and backups are handled through the destination selected in the iOS share interface. Settings → Clear All Data deletes local tracker data, temporary export-cache copies and scheduled app reminders, but does not delete an App Store purchase.

The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by USDA FNA, Puerto Rico ADSEF, any SNAP/PAN or WIC agency, retailer or card issuer. It does not provide official balances, eligibility decisions, retailer acceptance or product authorization. Official-source and non-affiliation information is available in Help / Support.
